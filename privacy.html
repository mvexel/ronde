Ronde: privacy policy

Ronde is a personal Android app (phone and Wear OS watch) that plays planned workouts from intervals.icu and uploads the recordings back to intervals.icu.

  • What it accesses: with your permission (OAuth scopes CALENDAR:READ and ACTIVITY:WRITE) Ronde reads your planned workouts and uploads activities you record with it.
  • Where data goes: only between your phone, your watch and intervals.icu. Ronde has no server of its own, no analytics, no ads, and shares nothing with third parties.
  • What is stored: your intervals.icu access token, encrypted with the Android Keystore on your phone; synced workouts and your recordings, on your phone and watch.
  • Removing access: log out in Ronde, or revoke the app at https://intervals.icu/settings/apps. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.
